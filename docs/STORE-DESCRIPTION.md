## Full description

<h3>LightTab — a quiet new tab for people who want to get something done</h3>

<p>LightTab replaces your new-tab page with a calm, personal start page. Your data stays in
your browser: no account needed, no tracking, no ads, and it works offline out of the box.
The only permission it asks for is <b>storage</b>.</p>

<ul>
  <li><b>Live clock &amp; date</b> — 24h/12h, seconds, three clock faces, and an optional
      second timezone. The Chinese lunar calendar (offline, 1900–2100) is built in.</li>
  <li><b>Search that launches</b> — type to search your saved sites locally, get engine
      suggestions, run quick math, or press Enter to jump straight to an exact shortcut.</li>
  <li><b>Shortcuts grid</b> — drag to reorder (mouse or touch), group into folders, organise
      by tags, paste a whole list to add many at once, import from browser bookmarks. Brand
      icons are baked in — no favicon requests leave your browser.</li>
  <li><b>To-dos</b> — with optional due dates that show up on the month calendar, and
      overdue highlights.</li>
  <li><b>Widgets you choose</b> — weather (Open-Meteo, no API key), countdown, pomodoro,
      movie of the day, wallpapers with a quote shuffle. Hide any of them.</li>
  <li><b>Wallpapers</b> — bundled gradient art plus your own uploads; optionally fetch a
      curated online library (off by default).</li>
  <li><b>Make it yours</b> — dark / light / follow-system themes, a custom accent colour,
      and a bilingual interface (English / 中文).</li>
  <li><b>Back up &amp; sync on your terms</b> — export/import JSON, merge-import shortcuts,
      opt-in self-hosted cloud sync (off by default), and optional local backup reminders.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Privacy</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Zero tracking, zero analytics, zero ads.</li>
  <li>Required permission: <b>storage</b> only.</li>
  <li>Optional permissions are requested only when you use a feature
      (bookmark import / "add current tab").</li>
  <li>Search suggestions (on by default, switchable off) go straight to your chosen engine;
      with them off, no keystroke leaves the device until you search.</li>
  <li>Optional features that touch the network are always off until you enable them:
      cloud sync, the online wallpaper library, weather, and (default-off) local error capture
      which never leaves the device.</li>
</ul>
